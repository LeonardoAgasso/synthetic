How it works

What we collect

Synthetic collects cases where a brand used AI-generated people in its communication: models in campaigns and product photos, people in video ads, digital twins of real models, virtual influencers. We also list brands that have publicly pledged not to.

When a case is added

A case is added only if the brand disclosed it, in a post, a press release or a note in the ad itself, or confirmed it to a news outlet. Every entry has at least one source.

We don’t publish suspicions, image analysis or unconfirmed reports, even when they seem plausible.

What the register doesn’t say

The register describes facts, not judgements. Being listed doesn’t mean a brand did anything unlawful or wrong: it means it used this technology and said so.

Reporting a case

Open a report with a link to the source. We check it before adding it to the register.

Report a brand

Corrections and removals

If an entry is wrong, incomplete or out of date, request a correction. Anyone can, brands included. Every change stays visible in the repository’s public history.

You can also email us.

Images and trademarks

We don’t publish campaign images: we always link to the original sources. Brand names are used only to identify them, and the project isn’t affiliated with any of them.

Open data

The full register can be downloaded as JSON, and as CSV from the main page.

Privacy

The site uses no cookies, analytics or external fonts, and stores nothing in your browser. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes.
